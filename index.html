About the project

Built by a student, for students.

Finding a faculty member should not mean asking three people and checking four floors. Faculty Finder puts seat, room and contact details in one fast, mobile-friendly page.

It is an independent, unofficial project. Details come from available seating and contact lists, so some may be out of date. Always confirm important meetings with the department.

Spotted a wrong seat or email, or want your own details corrected or removed? Use the report link on any faculty page, or write to us.
